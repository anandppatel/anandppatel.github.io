THE ALGEBRA BEHIND THE PICTURE

Av = λv, with v ≠ 0.

An eigenvector is a nonzero vector whose image is a scalar multiple of itself. The scalar λ is its eigenvalue.

Positive λ preserves the direction; negative λ reverses it. The length is multiplied by |λ|. When λ = 0, the image is the zero vector, which has no direction.
