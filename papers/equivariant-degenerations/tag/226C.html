Principle 2.13. (226C)

Suppose $\gamma_{i}$, $1\leq i\leq n$ are $1$-parameter families, full for $\alpha$, and suppose each pair $\gamma_{i},\gamma_{j}$ satisfy the hypothesis in Lemma 2.11 [4DEC]. Then, the equivariant class $$\p_{\mathcal{X}_{u}}-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\p_{\mathcal{X}^{\gamma_{i}}_{0}}$$ can be represented by a nonnegative sum of equivariant fundamental classes of effective cycles.

If, in addition, the predegrees of the hypersurfaces $\mathcal{X}^{\gamma_{1}}_{0},\ldots,\mathcal{X}^{\gamma_{n}}_0$ add up to the predegree of $\mathcal{X}_{u}$, then,

$$\p_{\mathcal{X}_u}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\p_{\mathcal{X}^{\gamma_{i}}_{0}}.$$
