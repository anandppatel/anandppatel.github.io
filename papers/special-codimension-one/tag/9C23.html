Let $\cA$ be any vector bundle on $\mathbb{P}^1_{t} \times \mathbb{P}^1_{s}$ such that $\cA_{t}$ has vanishing higher cohomology for all $t \in \P^{1}_{t}$. We can compute the degree of the pushforward $f_{t*}\cA$ using the Grothendieck-Riemann-Roch formula; we get: $$\deg \, (f_{t*}\cA) = c_1\cA\cdot R_s + \frac{c_1^2\cA}{2}-c_2\cA.$$

Now consider the sequence which gives rise to the vector bundle $\cD$: $$0 \longrightarrow f_{t}^*f_{t*}\cW(-(l+1)R_s) \longrightarrow \cW(-(l+1)R_s) \longrightarrow \cD \longrightarrow 0$$ The family of bundles $\mathbb{P}\cD_{t}$, $t \in \P^{1}_{t}$, is the family of directrices of $\mathbb{P}\cW_t$. If we let $\eta$ be the hyperplane class associated to the $\cO(1)$ on $\mathbb{P}\cW(-(l+1)R_s)$, we get the equality $$[\mathbb{P}\cD] = \eta^{N-1-r} +(a+l)R_t\cdot \eta^{N-2-r}$$ in the Chow ring of $\mathbb{P}\cW(-(l+1)R_s)$.
