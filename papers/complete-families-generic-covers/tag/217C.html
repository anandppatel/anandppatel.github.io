Definition 3.9. (217C)

Let $M_{d}$ denote the set of ordered pairs $(\Lambda,\eta)$ satisfying the following:

  1. $\Lambda$ is a sub-lattice of $\Z^{2}$ of index $d$, and

  2. $\Z^{2}/\Lambda$ is a non-trivial cyclic group with $\eta \in \Z^{2}/\Lambda$ a generator.
