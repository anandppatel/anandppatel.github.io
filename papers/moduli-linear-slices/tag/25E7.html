Proposition 4.5. (25E7)

If $C$ is a reduced plane curve and $T_{\PP^2}(-\log C)$ has a section, then $C$ is equivalent to a union of orbits under one of the two actions by $\mathbb{G}_m$ and $\mathbb{G}_a$ as follows:

Explicitly, there are two cases:

  1. $C$ is projectively equivalent to a union of curves of the form $X^{p} Y^{q}=cZ^{p+q}$, $c\in \mathbb{C}^{\times}$, and possibly a subset of the three coordinate lines.
  2. $C$ is projectively equivalent to a union of members of the family $\{XZ-Y^2+cZ^2\mid c\in \mathbb{C}\}$ of conics quadritangent to $\{XZ-Y^2=0\}$ at $[0:0:1]$, and possibly the line $\{Z=0\}$.
