Corollary 3.7. (BB42)

Let $i : X_{b} \hookrightarrow X$ denote the inclusion of the fiber over $b \in B$. Then the sheaf $i^{-1}\ker \nabla$ is a locally constant sheaf of free rank $r$ $\Oo_{B,b}$-modules on $X_{b}$ (in the Euclidean topology).
