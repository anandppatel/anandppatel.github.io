Theorem 7.7. (638D)

If $m \geq \lfloor (g+d-1)/(d-1) \rfloor$, then Conjecture 7.1 [41DC] for $\td \U_g(\F_m, d\tau)$ implies Conjecture 7.5 [B7D9] for $\td{\H}_{d,g}^{\dagger}$. If $m \geq \lceil 2(g+d-1)/d \rceil$, then Conjecture 7.1 [41DC] for $\td{\U}_g(\F_m, d\tau)$ is equivalent to Conjecture 7.5 [B7D9] for $\td{\H}_{d,g}^{\dagger}$.
