Proof. (A154)

The irreducibility statement follows from Theorem 2.10 [B311]. To show that $M(E[2])$ is the ramification locus of $\mu$, consider $[\alpha \from C \to \P^1] \in \H_{d,g}$ and the map of sheaves: $$0 \to \alpha^{*}(T_{\P^1}) \to T_C \to N_{\alpha}\to 0.$$ The tangent space to $\H_{d,g}$ at $\alpha$ is $H^0(C, N_\alpha)/\alpha^*H^0(\P^1, T_{\P^1})$ and the tangent space to $\M_g$ at $C$ is $H^1(C, T_C)$. The map $$d\mu \from H^{0}(C, N_{\alpha})/\alpha^{*}H^{0}(\P^1, T_{\P^1}) \to H^{1}(C, T_C)$$ fails to be surjective precisely when $H^1(C, \alpha^*T_{\P^1}) \neq 0$, that is, when $\alpha \in M(E[2])$.
