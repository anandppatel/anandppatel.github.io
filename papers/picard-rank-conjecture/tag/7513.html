Remark 7.6. (7513)

It is easy to see that the classes of $T$, $D$, and $\Delta$ can be expressed as $\Q$-linear combinations of $\lambda_h$, $\kappa_h$, and $\xi_h$ and vice versa. Also, by Proposition 2.3 [E425], the framed/unframed distinction is irrelevant. Therefore, Conjecture 7.5 [B7D9] is equivalent to the Picard rank conjecture stated in the introduction, namely that $$\Pic_\Q \H_{d,g} = 0.$$
