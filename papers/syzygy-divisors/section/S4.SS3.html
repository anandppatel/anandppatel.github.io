4.3. The computation

We now compute $\ch N_i$ using the expression in Proposition 4.2 [E90D]. Since we are ultimately only interested in $\c_1$ and $\ch_2$, we ignore all terms of degree higher than $2$. We may assume, for example, that the computation is happening over a general curve $B \to \widetilde {\orb H}_{d,g}$. Denote by $\pi \from C \to B$ and $p \from P \to B$ the two projections.

From Proposition 4.2 [E90D], we have $$\begin{split} \ch N_i &= \sum_{j=0}^{i+1} (-1)^{j-i-1} \ch \left(K_{i+1}(j)\right)\\ &= \left(\sum_{j=0}^{i+1}(-1)^{j-1} \ch(\wedge^{i+1-j}E) \ch (\alpha_* \omega_\alpha^j)) \right) - \ch(\wedge^i E) + \ch(\wedge^{i+1}E) \ch(E^\vee). \end{split}$$ The two correction terms at the end are needed because the $j = 0$ and $j = 1$ terms in the summation are differ from the corresponding terms of the Koszul resolution in the following way (the computation is in the K-ring):

Next, by Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch applied to $\alpha$ we get $$\ch \alpha_* \omega_\alpha^\ell = \alpha_* \left(1 + \ell \cdot \c_1(\omega_\alpha) + \frac{\ell^2\c_1(\omega_\alpha)^2}{2}\right) \left(1 - \frac{\c_1(\omega_\alpha)^2}{2} + \frac{\c_1(\omega_\alpha)^2+c_2(\Omega_{C/P})}{12}\right).$$ Note that $\c_1(\omega_\alpha)$ is the class of the ramification divisor of $\alpha \from C \to P$. In particular, $\alpha_* \c_1(\omega_\alpha)$ is the class of the branch divisor, which is cut out by a section of $(\det E)^{\otimes 2}$. Therefore, we get $$\alpha_* \c_1(\omega_\alpha) = 2\c_1 E.$$ Specializing (4.3) to the case $\ell = 0$ and comparing the degree two terms yields $$\ch_2 E = \alpha_*\left(\frac{\c_1(\omega_\alpha)^2+c_2(\Omega_{C/P})}{12}\right).$$ After using (4.4) and (4.5) to simplify (4.3), we get $$\ch \alpha_* \omega_\alpha^\ell = d + (2\ell-1)\c_1(E) + \left(\ch_2(E) + \frac{\ell^2+\ell}{2}\pi_*\c_1(\omega_\alpha)^2 \right).$$

The result is the following: $$\begin{split} \ch_0 (N_i) &= \frac{i(d-2-i)}{d-1} {d \choose {i+1}} \\ \c_1 (N_i) &= (d-2-i) {{d-2} \choose {i-1}} \c_1(E)\\ \ch_2 (N_i) &= {{d-4} \choose {i-1}} \left(d \ch_2(E) + \frac{(d-4)i+2}{2(d-i-1)}\c_1^2(E) - \c_1(\omega_\alpha)^2 \right) \end{split}$$

We use this computation to finish a postponed proof from Proposition 2.4 [AB0E].

Corollary 4.3. (BFA5)

$\deg N_i = (d-2-i) (g+d-1) {{d-2} \choose i-1}.$

Proof. (176C)

Follows from (4.7) and that $\deg \c_1(E) = (g+d-1)$.
