Proof. (702A)

We have the birational isomorphism

Note that $q \in \Sym^2S$ spans the kernel of the natural surjection $\pi \from \Sym^2 S \to \Sym^4 V$. So the claimed birational isomorphism is given by sending a two dimensional subspace $L \subset \Sym^4 V$ to the image of $\pi^{-1}L$ in $\Sym^2S/q$.
