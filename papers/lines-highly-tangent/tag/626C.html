Definition 2.7. (626C)

Let $X \subset \P^{n}$ be a degree $d$ hypersurface. For each $k \leq d$ define the variety

to be the locus of pairs $(\ell, x)$ with $x \in X$, such that $\ell$ meets $X$ at the point $x$ to order $\geq k$.
