Section 4. Intersection Theory computation

Theorem 4.1. (0122)

For $d\geq 5$, the number of 2-planes in a smooth 4-fold is at most $120 d^2 - 150 d^3 + 35 d^4$.

Proof. (1742)

Let $S$ be the tautological subbundle of the Grassmannian $\mathbb{G}(1,5)$ and $Z_{5,1}\subset \mathbb{P}(S)\times_{\mathbb{G}(1,5)}\mathbb{P}(S)$ consist of the locus $(\ell,p_1,p_2)$ where $\ell$ is a line in $\mathbb{P}^5$ meeting $X$ at points $p_1$ and $p_2$ with orders 5 and 1, respectively.

By Theorem 3.8 [81FA], $Z_{5,1}$ is 4-dimensional. Let $H$ be the $\mathscr{O}(1)$ class of $\mathbb{P}(S)$, which is also the hyperplane class of $\mathbb{P}^5$ pulled back under $\mathbb{P}(S)\to \mathbb{P}^5$. Let $H_1$ and $H_2$ be $H$ pulled back to the two factors of $\mathbb{P}(S)\times_{\mathbb{G}(1,5)}\mathbb{P}(S)$ respectively under the projections $\pi_1,\pi_2: \mathbb{P}(S)\times_{\mathbb{G}(1,5)}\mathbb{P}(S)\to \mathbb{P}(S)$.

To compute $Z_{5,1}\subset \mathbb{P}(S)\times_{\mathbb{G}(1,5)}\mathbb{P}(S)$, we note that it is the zero locus of a section of the sheaf of relative principal parts $\pi_1^{*}P_{\mathbb{P}(S)/\mathbb{G}(1,5)}^4(\mathscr{O}(d))\times \pi_2^{*}\mathscr{O}(d)$ as defined in [5, Section 11.1.1]. The relative canonical divisor of $\mathbb{P}(S)\to \mathbb{G}(1,5)$ is $-2H+\sigma_1$. Therefore, the class of $Z_{5,1}$ is

inside $A^{\bullet}(\mathbb{P}(S)\times_{\mathbb{G}(1,5)}\mathbb{P}(S))$ by [5, Theorem 11.2].

We will intersect $[Z_{5,1}]\in A^{\bullet}(\mathbb{P}(S)\times_{\mathbb{G}(1,5)}\mathbb{P}(S))$ with the pullback of $\mathbb{G}(1,4)\cong \sigma_{1,1}\subset \mathbb{G}(1,5)$ and the product $H_1H_2$. This must be nonnegative on each component of $Z_{5,1}$ by Kleiman's transversality [5, Theorem 1.7] and yields

In the final integral, the image of $H_1$ in $A^{\bullet}((\mathbb{P}(S)\times_{\mathbb{G}(1,5)}\mathbb{P}(S))|_{\mathbb{G}(1,4)})$ can be identified as the image of $H_1$ under the map $A^{\bullet}(\mathbb{P}^5)\to A^{\bullet}((\mathbb{P}(S)\times_{\mathbb{G}(1,5)}\mathbb{P}(S))|_{\mathbb{G}(1,4)})$ in the commutative diagram of pullback maps of Chow rings

Since $H_1^5=0$ in $A^{\bullet}(\mathbb{P}^4)$, the same is true in $A^{\bullet}((\mathbb{P}(S)\times_{\mathbb{G}(1,5)}\mathbb{P}(S))|_{\mathbb{G}(1,4)})$. Therefore, the numerical answer in (4.1) is $O(d^4)$.
