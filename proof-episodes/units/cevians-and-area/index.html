Cevians and area

Trigonometric Ceva, symmedians, Gergonne and Nagel, and Heron’s formula.

10 problems, 40 parts. Optional hints and 7 diagrams accompany the assignment.

Read the homework →Download Markdown

Proof episodes

  1. 11Trigonometric Ceva

    Turn side ratios into sine ratios, keeping both directions of the criterion.

  2. 12The symmedian point

    Reflect the medians and relate the result to squared side ratios.

  3. 13The Nagel point

    Construct the excircles and prove concurrence of the opposite-contact cevians.

  4. 14The Gergonne point

    Equal incircle tangent lengths make Ceva’s product cancel.

  5. 15Heron’s formula

    An incircle and an excircle lead to the area in terms of three side lengths.

Review ordinary Ceva, angle bisectors, and the triangle-center episodes as needed. The assignment also uses reflections and equal tangent lengths from The Basics.
