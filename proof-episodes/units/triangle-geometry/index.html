Triangle geometry

Triangle centers, areas, the Euler line, Thales, the nine-point circle, and ordinary Ceva.

10 problems, 31 parts. Optional hints and 6 diagrams accompany the assignment.

Proof episodes

  1. 01The centroid

    An area proof that the medians meet, with the 2:1 division ratio.

  2. 02The circumcenter

    Equal distances identify the perpendicular bisectors and the circle through three vertices.

  3. 03The orthocenter

    An auxiliary triangle turns the three altitudes into perpendicular bisectors.

  4. 04The incenter and incircle

    Angle bisectors give equal distances to the sides and a tangent circle.

  5. 05The Euler line

    Construct a candidate on OG, then identify it as the orthocenter.

  6. 06The angle-bisector theorem

    Compare areas to obtain the side ratio, then prove the converse.

  7. 07Thales’ theorem

    A diameter sees a right angle; the converse identifies the circle.

  8. 08The nine-point circle: six midpoints

    A dilation puts the side midpoints and vertex–orthocenter midpoints on one circle.

  9. 09The nine-point circle: altitude feet

    Thales brings the three altitude feet onto the circle from Part I.

  10. 10Ceva’s theorem

    Areas establish the concurrence criterion in both directions.
